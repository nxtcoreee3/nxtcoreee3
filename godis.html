what it is

About godis

An experimental tool for device detection, staged MobileGestalt tweaks, `.godis` profiles, backups and restore, PosterBoard collections, and supported respring workflows.

Supported versionsiOS 27 developer beta 1 through beta 4 are the primary MobileGestalt targets; Bad Poster mode targets iOS 26–27.0 beta 4
InstallXcode plus a signing or sideloading method; Developer Mode may be required
Bundle IDcom.nxtcoreee3.godis
FeaturesMobileGestalt tweaks, device detection, profiles, `.godis` import/export, backups, Bad Poster mode, diagnostics, and respring
WarningWrites system-managed data. Back up first; unsupported versions may be unstable or require a restore

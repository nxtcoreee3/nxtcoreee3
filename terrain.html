quick summary

Customize wallpapers, device settings, and more from one iOS app.

Terrain brings PosterBoard wallpaper management and a collection of iOS customization tools into one place.

about the app

What Terrain does

Browse and manage custom PosterBoard wallpaper packs, work with device customization options, and access files and themes through Terrain’s built-in tools.

WallpapersImport `.tendies` packs, browse the integrated catalogs, preview packs, and select wallpapers to apply.
CustomizationIncludes MobileGestalt and other device customization tools. Feature availability depends on your iOS version.
More toolsIncludes a file browser, theme utilities, and additional iOS-focused features.
Before useBack up your device first. Review the project’s current compatibility notes; some features are limited on certain iOS releases.

project background

About this fork

Terrain is a community fork of Erosion, an iOS customization toolbox. It is rebranded and maintained by nxtcoreee3, with Terrain-specific changes such as the integrated nxtcoreee3 wallpaper source.

Temporarily discontinued

WaffleStore is no longer operational. Apple changed its authentication backend, so the method WaffleStore relied on can no longer be used. The app will not work until an alternative backend solution is found.

from the project file

What WaffleStore was

WaffleStore was a jailed app store app downgrader for iOS 16.4 and later. It did not use exploits and was based on the work of MuffinStoreJailed and PancakeStore.

PlatformiPhone and iPad running iOS 16.4 or later
CategoryJailed app store downgrader
RequirementApple ID with two-factor authentication and a sideloading method
StatusRepository remains available for reference; app functionality is unavailable

Do not rely on old builds

Use WaffleStore at your own risk. Old copies may fail during authentication and could cause app-data loss or other damage. Do not contact the project expecting the discontinued backend to work.
